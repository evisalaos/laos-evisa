Laos eVisa Online: Electronic Travel Authorization Overview

Prepare for your journey to the Lao People's Democratic Republic with clear information to Apply Lao eVisa online, designated checkpoints, and passport rules.

Get Lao eVisa Online

The Laos eVisa is an electronic travel authorization issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Lao People's Democratic Republic for eligible international visitors. Approved applicants receive an official travel letter permitting entry at designated airports and land checkpoints.

Understanding the Electronic Authorization System for Laos

International travelers planning a short stay in Laos can submit an electronic visa request prior to departure. Understanding the benefits of the Laos eVisa system helps visitors prepare accurate documentation and select an approved arrival port.

  • Single-entry travel authorization valid for 60 days from the date of issuance.
  • Permits a stay of up to 30 days in Laos for tourism or personal visits.
  • Accepted at Wattay International Airport, Luang Prabang International Airport, and Pakse Airport.
  • Recognized at designated Thai-Lao Friendship Bridges and Boten checkpoints.

Applicants upload a passport bio-data scan and a compliant portrait photo online following Laos travel visa requirements, then print the approved travel letter to present at border inspection.

Selecting the Appropriate Laos Travel Category

Before submitting an online form, visitors must confirm which category matches their travel purpose. Reviewing the types of eVisas available for Laos ensures tourists and family visitors comply with national immigration rules.

  • Tourist eVisa covers sightseeing, cultural tours, and recreational holidays.
  • Visit eVisa supports short family reunions and social visits.
  • Every traveler, including infants and children, requires an individual approval letter.
  • Passports must remain valid for at least six months beyond the arrival date.

Travelers planning academic enrollment, diplomatic missions, long-term residence, or employment must secure specialized authorization through Lao authorities prior to arrival.

Preparing for Arrival at Lao Border Checkpoints

Upon reaching an approved checkpoint in Laos, travelers who get Laos tourist eVisa approval present their original passport and printed electronic approval letter at the designated eVisa lane for verification and stamping.

  • Confirm your passport has at least two blank visa pages for official entry stamps.
  • Verify that all personal details on the approval letter match your passport exactly.
  • Keep your initial hotel address and onward travel booking accessible for inspection.
  • Check your entry stamp at the booth to confirm the permitted departure date.

Carrying two printed paper copies of your approval letter alongside your passport ensures smooth airline boarding and fast immigration clearance.

Planning a Trip Between Laos and Thailand

Some visitors combine a holiday in Laos with time in Thailand, using Bangkok for a flight connection or a Friendship Bridge for an overland crossing. Entry requirements for the two countries are separate, so a Laos eVisa does not provide permission to enter Thailand.
